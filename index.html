📘 3D Model Guide

Welcome! Here’s how to navigate the 3D dashboard:

- Top left: Change the base map (OpenStreetMap, Satellite, or no base map).
- Below the base map: Use the arrows to move the view up, down, left, or right.
- Bottom left: Toggle map layers on or off to see exactly what you want.
- Top right: Check the weather widget and use zoom in/out buttons.
- Sun slider: Adjusts sunlight from 6 AM to 6 PM, showing how sunlight hits the buildings.
